Scoring rules

  • Group stage: 3 pts per win, 1 pt per draw, +5 pts for finishing first in the group.
  • Knockouts: +5 pts for each round reached (Round of 32, Round of 16, quarter-final, semi-final, final), and +5 pts for winning the whole thing.
  • Player score = sum of their 3 teams. Ties broken by combined goal difference, then combined goals scored.
  • Wooden spoon: single worst team — fewest group-stage points, then worst goal difference, then most goals against.
